Daymark User Guide

Everything you need to build a calm, sustainable daily rhythm.

The three mark states

Daymark is built for real human schedules—not perfectionism. Every habit day can hold one of three clear states:

  • Stamped (Done) — Check off a habit in Today or click a square in Monthly Grid. Builds your rhythm and streak.
  • Rest Day (Streak Protected) — Tap the moon icon in Today (or Shift+click / right-click a cell in Monthly Grid). Ideal for illness, travel, or planned recovery: keeps your streak alive and excludes that day from your target score.
  • Off-Schedule Day — When you schedule a habit for specific weekdays (e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri), other days appear gently dashed and never break your streak.

True cross-month streaks & mid-day grace

Your streaks carry smoothly across month boundaries (never resetting on the 1st), and your active streak stays alive throughout today until midnight so mornings never start at zero.

Today view vs. Monthly Grid

Use Today for calm 10-second daily check-ins grouped by Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Anytime (with arrow buttons to step back to yesterday). Switch to Monthly Grid anytime to scan real calendar weeks, spot patterns, and bulk-update days.

Mood, Sleep & Rhythm Insights

Log your daily Mood (1–10) and Sleep (in 30-minute increments). Once you have a few logged days, Rhythm Insights automatically surfaces plain-English patterns—like how 7+ hours of sleep lifts your completion rate or which weekday is your strongest.

Unified Daily Note & Prompt Tags

Jot a highlight, gratitude, or lesson in 30 seconds. Tap prompt pills to beat blank-page friction, add your own custom tags, or search past notes from the Monthly Grid view. Clicking any day number in the grid header opens that date’s note.

Editing, Archiving & Undo

Click the pencil icon on any habit to rename it, swap its icon, or adjust its weekday schedule—your past marks stay untouched. Done with a habit for now? Choose Archive instead of Delete to hide it from Today while preserving all historical stats. Deleted or cleared something by accident? Click Undo in the notification toast.

Monthly Keepsake & Studio Preferences

Click Keepsake to preview and download or copy a letterpress summary PNG matched to your current theme. Open ⋯ → Studio Preferences to switch between Linen Paper (light), Evening Ink (dark), three Studio Ink accents, Sunday/Monday week start, dot-grid paper, and your personal motto.

Local-first privacy & optional Cloud Sync

Daymark works 100% offline in your browser with no account required. If you want cross-device sync, click Cloud Sync to sign in—offline edits merge safely without overwriting local marks. On shared computers, signing out lets you choose whether to keep or wipe local browser data.

Backups, CSV Export & Privacy Blur

Open ⋯ to download a full JSON Backup (all months, schedules, journals, and preferences) or restore one on a new browser, or export the active month to spreadsheet-safe CSV (including Daily Notes). Tap the eye icon (or press P) to softly blur habit names and personal notes when using Daymark in public.
